Mnemosyne

A personal media library and planner for Obsidian.

Mnemosyne is a private, single-user tool. It organises the owner's own media library (saved posts, images, videos, references) and shows a daily planner inside Obsidian.

What it does with your Google account

  • Google Calendar (read-only) — shows today's events in the planner.
  • Google Tasks — shows and updates the owner's task list.
  • Gmail (read-only) — shows a short list of recent messages in the planner.
  • Google Drive (only files created by the app) — keeps the app's own sync files.

Data stays on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
