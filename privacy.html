Privacy policy

Mnemosyne · last updated 17 October 2026

Who uses the app

Mnemosyne is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar events — read-only, to show them in the planner.
  • Google Tasks — to show and update the owner's tasks.
  • Gmail messages — read-only, to show recent messages in the planner. The app never sends, deletes or changes e-mail.
  • Google Drive — only files the app itself created.

How the data is stored

All data is processed and stored locally on the owner's own devices. Sign-in tokens are kept locally and can be removed at any time by signing out or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
